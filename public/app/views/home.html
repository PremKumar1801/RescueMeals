How RescueMeals Works

A simple, transparent bridge connecting surplus food sources directly with local communities in need.

1

1. Post Surplus

Restaurants, caterers, bakeries, and households list excess cooked meals or raw ingredients in seconds.

2

2. Match & Request

Verified community centers, shelters, and volunteers browse nearby listings and claim matching meals.

3

3. Deliver & Feed

Direct pickup and distribution ensures food arrives fresh, safe, and ready to nourish families immediately.
